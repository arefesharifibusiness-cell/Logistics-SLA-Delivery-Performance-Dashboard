SLA rules. Pickup: the parcel is collected inside the 2-hour window the customer booked; late = after the window ends (collecting early is tracked separately, it is not a breach). Delivery: the parcel is signed within 180 minutes of the courier receiving it (an assumption: change it in the filter bar and check it against the business SLA before drawing conclusions). Hours and days use the time the task was completed. Pickup and delivery files share no order or courier IDs, so the two stages are compared side by side, not followed parcel by parcel.
